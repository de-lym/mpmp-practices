Vocabulary of Control

No word here is colored by whether its cases were "protective" or "repressive," since every act of restriction can claim either. Instead, each word is classified by how concentrated and unaccountable the deciding authority was, represented through color. Cream means process, appeal, or oversight existed; black means accountability was mixed or contested; red means authority was concentrated with little independent check. Words stay invisible until they cross the selected threshold boundary, so drag the lever yourself to declassify more, or less, and click a block to see which cases it belongs to.
