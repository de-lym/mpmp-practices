Vocabulary of Control

No word here is colored by whether its cases were "protective" or "repressive," since every act of restriction can claim either. Color tracks how checked the deciding authority was. Cream means process, appeal, or oversight existed. Black means accountability was mixed or contested. Red means authority was concentrated with little independent check. Words stay invisible until they cross your threshold, so drag the line to declassify more, or less. Click a box to see which cases it belongs to.
